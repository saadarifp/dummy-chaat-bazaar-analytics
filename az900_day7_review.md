# AZ-900 Day 7 Review Notes

Quick recall list covering Days 2 to 6. One line each, gaps marked after the practice test.

Cloud computing: services over the internet, pay for use. Shared responsibility: provider secures the datacenter, customer owns data and access, middle shifts by model.
IaaS rent infrastructure, manage OS up. PaaS bring app and data. SaaS just use software. CapEx own upfront, OpEx pay as you go.
Region: geographic area of datacenters. Availability Zone: independent datacenter within a region. Region pair: distant partner region for disaster recovery.
Hierarchy: Management Group, Subscription, Resource Group, Resources. Policy inherits downward. Subscription is the billing boundary. Deleting a resource group deletes contents.
Compute: VM full control, Scale Sets auto-add VMs, App Service managed web, Functions event-triggered serverless, Containers portable packaging, AKS orchestrates them, AVD streamed desktops.
Storage: Blob objects, Files SMB shares, Queue messages, Table NoSQL, Managed Disks for VMs. Tiers Hot, Cool, Cold, Archive by access frequency. Redundancy LRS one datacenter, ZRS across zones, GRS paired region, GZRS both.
Identity: Entra ID is the identity service. Authentication proves who, authorization decides what. MFA second proof. Conditional Access rules on sign in. RBAC roles to users at scopes. Least privilege minimum needed. Zero Trust verify explicitly, assume breach. Defender for Cloud posture and threat protection.
