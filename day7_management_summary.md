# Management Summary

Based on my analysis, these are the 3 things management should know.

**1. A quarter of our revenue never reaches us.** Discounts and delivery commissions consume 23.9% of gross sales, and commission alone is three quarters of that. Action: make channel cost a monthly KPI and push customers toward Direct ordering.

**2. Talabat is our most expensive channel.** It carries our highest volume at the highest rate, taking 59% of all commission we pay. Worse, half of all leakage comes from orders where we discount and pay commission on the same sale. Action: stop discounting aggregator orders; run promotions on Direct only.

**3. Barsha is underperforming on basket, not footfall.** Its average order is AED 19 against a company AED 40, and it sells no Mains at all. Action: fix the Barsha menu mix before considering bigger decisions.
